# Weak → strong examples

Fictional candidate: **Ana**, backend engineer, 5 years, Kotlin and TypeScript, health-tech and education. Use these to show the standard, never as content for the real candidate.

## 1. Tell me about yourself

**Weak** (long, no direction, translated):
> "So, I started in 2020 in a company of hospitals software, there I worked with Kotlin, and after that I went to another company where we did many things, microservices, and also front-end sometimes, and now I am in a startup working with AI and I'm very passionate about technology and I like to learn new things always."

**Strong** (main point first, specifics, ends on the role):
> "I'm a backend engineer with five years of experience, mostly Kotlin and TypeScript. Most recently I built the scheduling API for an online-tutoring platform, which handles about forty thousand lessons a month. Before that I spent three years on hospital software, where I rebuilt the lab-results sync so doctors saw results in minutes instead of hours. Now I'm looking for a product team where I can own backend features end to end, which is why this role caught my attention."

Why it works: four sentences, two concrete results, ends by connecting to the job.

## 2. A number under follow-up

**Weak:**
> "We reduced no-shows by 40%." — "40% of what?" — "Uh, of the appointments, more or less."

**Strong:**
> "After we added SMS reminders, missed appointments went from 15% to 9% of all bookings, measured over the next three months against the three months before."

Why it works: the number has a definition, a baseline and a time frame. If you can't say all three, don't use the number.

## 3. A gap

**Weak** (overclaim):
> "Yes, I have experience with Kubernetes." — "How did you handle a failing readiness probe?" — silence.

**Strong:**
> "I haven't run Kubernetes in production. Our services ran on a managed PaaS, so I know health checks, rolling deploys and autoscaling from that side. I'd expect a short ramp-up on the Kubernetes specifics."

## 4. Salary pushback

**Weak:**
> "I'd like 6,500 per month." — "Our budget is closer to 5,500." — "Ah, OK, 5,500 is fine too."

**Strong:**
> "I'd like 6,500 per month." — "Our budget is closer to 5,500." — "I understand. Given the scope and my experience running high-traffic APIs, I'm comfortable at 6,500. I'm happy to talk about the full package."

## 5. Why are you leaving?

**Weak:**
> "The company was very disorganized and my manager didn't listen."

**Strong:**
> "I've grown a lot where I am, but the product is moving into maintenance mode. I want to keep building new features, and your roadmap for the next year is exactly that kind of work."
