# Building the roadmap

The roadmap is a checklist in `roadmap.md`. Each item: checkbox, what, time estimate, done criterion. Put the interview date/time (candidate's time zone) and "time left" at the top. Order items so the most valuable ones come first; if time runs out, the candidate has done the important part.

## Size by time left

### Under 24 hours

1. Research the company: 20 min. Done: 3 facts to mention + 2 questions to ask.
2. Pitch ("tell me about yourself"): 30 min. Done: under 90 seconds, said out loud twice without reading.
3. 5 key answers for the stage: 45 min. Done: each in notes.md, 3–4 sentences, confirmed facts only.
4. Salary line: 10 min. Done: one number, one pushback answer.
5. One voice mock: 25 min. Done: result pasted, 2 fixes applied.
6. Questions to ask them: 10 min. Done: 3 questions from the research.
7. Interview-day checklist: see interview-day.md.

### 1 to 3 days

Everything above, plus:

8. Requirement → story map: 45 min. Done: every key requirement has a confirmed story or an honest gap line.
9. Close gaps.md: 30 min. Done: no open question about anything that's in an answer.
10. Stage question bank: 1h. Done: 10–15 likely questions answered out loud once.
11. 2–3 voice mocks with the feedback loop: 25 min each. Done: content ≥ 8/10 on the last one.

### 4 days or more

Everything above, plus:

12. Daily fluency drill (English interviews): 30–60 min/day. See english-plan.md.
13. Technical bank for the job's stack: 1h/day. See technical-prep.md.
14. Stage-specific practice (kata, system design): see technical-prep.md.
15. Final full mock the day before: 30 min. Done: fluency and content ≥ 8/10, no red flags.

Scale item times to the hours per day the candidate gave. If they have 1h/day for 5 days, don't plan 3h/day.

## Focus by stage

| Stage | Weight on | Mock type |
|---|---|---|
| HR / recruiter screen | pitch, motivation, why this company, salary, availability, English | HR mock |
| Technical | stack depth, real technical stories, trade-offs, AI usage in development, debugging | technical mock + technical-prep.md |
| Hiring manager | ownership, impact, collaboration, conflict, how they work, product sense | hiring-manager mock |
| Client (consultancy) | domain knowledge, communication, delivery, adapting to the client's process | client mock |
| Final / culture | values, motivation, long-term goals, questions to ask | final mock |
