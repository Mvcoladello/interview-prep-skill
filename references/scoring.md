# Scoring rubric

Every score must be backed by a quote from the transcript. If a topic wasn't covered, say "not covered" instead of scoring it. Voice assistants tend to score high; when the candidate pastes a result, re-check against the quotes and adjust.

## Fluency (0–2 each, total /10)

1. **Past tense** for past events
2. **No word-for-word translation** from the native language ("I made a research", "explain me")
3. **Short sentences**: one idea per sentence, no long chains of "and... and..."
4. **Few fillers and restarts** ("uh", "like", "you know", starting a sentence three times)
5. **Main point first**, then the example

Do not score pronunciation or accent. Only flag pronunciation when it changes meaning (e.g. "seventy" heard as "seven thousand").

## Content: HR / hiring manager (0–2 each, total /10)

1. **Fit**: connected their real experience to this role's requirements
2. **Specifics**: concrete examples, not adjectives ("I led 3 people and shipped in 6 weeks", not "I'm a great leader")
3. **Honesty**: plain gaps; every number explainable
4. **Ownership**: owner verbs ("I designed", "I led", "we shipped"), clear about their own part
5. **Salary / logistics**: held their number through one pushback; clear on availability and time zone

## Content: technical (0–2 each, total /14, also shown /10)

1. **Depth**: follow-ups went deeper with real details, not a repeat of the first answer
2. **Trade-offs**: explained why, alternatives and what would break
3. **Verification**: how they test and check work (including AI-generated code)
4. **Judgment**: named limits and risks
5. **Architecture**: idempotency, retries, failure handling, data consistency where relevant
6. **Honesty**: no overclaiming; numbers explainable
7. **Ownership**: clear about their own decisions

## Feedback block format

```
FLUENCY: x/10 (one line per criterion with a quote)
CONTENT: x/10 (one line per criterion with a quote)
TOP 2 FIXES:
1. You said: "..." → Better: "..."
2. You said: "..." → Better: "..."
REPEAT: one thing done well, with the exact words
RED FLAGS: yes/no for each, with quote
```

## Reading the scores

- Content < 7: work on stories and the requirement map, not English.
- Fluency < 7 with content ≥ 7: drills (english-plan.md), shorter sentences, rehearse the pitch out loud.
- Both ≥ 8 for two mocks in a row: ready. Stop over-rehearsing; do one light run the day of.
